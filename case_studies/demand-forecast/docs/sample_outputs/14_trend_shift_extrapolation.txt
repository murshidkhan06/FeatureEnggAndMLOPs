   99% of training sales are below 279 units.  Whatever k is, the largest tree forecast stays near its CEILING
   (the 'hgb_max_forecast' column barely moves) - that is the leaf-average limit.  The log-linear model keeps rising with
   the inputs, so its bias grows about half as fast; it still under-forecasts because Ridge shrinks its weights toward
   the training average.  Neither is perfect: the real cure for a new level is retraining (and scale-free targets).

D. ABLATION - give the tree a `trend_index` clock (days since 2023-01-01)
   drifted  hgb WAPE 0.196 bias -15.3%   |   hgb+trend_index WAPE 0.143 bias -5.0%
   normal   hgb WAPE 0.132 bias -4.3%   |   hgb+trend_index WAPE 0.145 bias +5.5%
   The clock lets the tree say 'late dates = newer, higher level' - but it can only repeat the LAST level it saw.
   It would fail again after the next jump; the principled fix is retraining (and scale-free targets).
