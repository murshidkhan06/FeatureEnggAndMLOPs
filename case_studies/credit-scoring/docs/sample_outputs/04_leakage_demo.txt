Takeaways
 * A too-good AUC (0.95+) in lending is a SMELL, not a success: ask 'when does this column exist?'
 * Fit imputers / scalers / encoders on TRAIN only (inside the Pipeline).  Effect is small for scaling, large for target-derived features.
 * Split by TIME and by APPLICANT; never by random row when customers can apply more than once.
