=== 5. cyclical encoding (optional): sin/cos of the weekday ===
  Monday    day_of_week=0: sin=+0.000  cos=+1.000
  Thursday  day_of_week=3: sin=+0.434  cos=-0.901
  Sunday    day_of_week=6: sin=-0.782  cos=+0.623
  distance Sunday->Monday = 0.868  (neighbours) vs Monday->Thursday = 1.950.  As plain numbers 6 and 0
  look 6 apart; on the circle they are adjacent.  December/January work the same with month_sin/month_cos.
