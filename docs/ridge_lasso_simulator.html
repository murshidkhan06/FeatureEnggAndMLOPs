Ridge vs Lasso — Interactive Regularization Simulator

Same penalty strength, two very different outcomes. Drag the slider and watch Lasso zero features out.

The dataset: 150 students, 6 candidate features predicting final_score. Three features genuinely matter (with different strengths); three are pure random noise with no real relationship to the score at all. Every feature is standardized (mean 0, std 1) so coefficients are directly comparable. Ridge penalizes the SUM OF SQUARES of the coefficients; Lasso penalizes the SUM OF ABSOLUTE VALUES — that one difference in the formula is the entire reason their behavior diverges so much.
