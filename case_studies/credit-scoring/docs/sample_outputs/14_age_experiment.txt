Age-band adverse impact ratio: 0.917 without age  ->  0.8647 with age
Young applicants (<25) approval rate: 0.8164 without age -> 0.7778 with age

DECISION RECORD (a POLICY decision owned by Compliance, laws differ by country):
  Evidence : adding age changes accuracy by a negligible amount but lowers the approval rate of the youngest band (<25).
  Default  : age EXCLUDED from the scoring model; age is kept only in the audit table (never sent to the API).
  Owner    : Compliance + Model Risk Committee.  Changing this requires a new registry version and a new review.
